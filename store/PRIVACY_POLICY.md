# Privacy Policy — HabitHatch

**Effective date:** August 4, 2026
**Last updated:** August 4, 2026

_This policy is published at https://github.com/WildanGhaly/habithatch/blob/main/store/PRIVACY_POLICY.md — use that link as your Play Console "Privacy policy" URL._

## 1. Who we are

HabitHatch ("**HabitHatch**", "**the app**", "**we**", "**us**") is a habit‑tracking mobile app published by **Wildan Ghaly**. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

**In one sentence: HabitHatch is offline‑first and does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your personal data.** Everything you create stays on your device.

## 2. The short version

- We do **not** require an account, sign‑up, or login.
- We do **not** collect, upload, or share your personal information.
- We do **not** use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third‑party marketing SDKs.
- All of your data (habits, streaks, coins, your companion, settings) is stored **locally on your device** and never leaves it.
- Reminders are delivered by your device as **local notifications**; no reminder data is sent anywhere.

## 3. Information we collect

**We do not collect any personal data.** HabitHatch has no servers and does not create an account for you.

The following information is created and stored **only on your device** so the app can work:

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored |
|------|---------|--------------------|
| Habits you create (name, category, schedule, reminder time) | Track and remind you of your habits | On your device (local database) |
| Progress (check‑offs, streaks, history, all‑clear days) | Show your stats and dashboards | On your device |
| In‑app economy (coins, garden, companion species, outfits, growth) | Run the game loop | On your device |
| App settings (theme, name you enter, notification preferences) | Personalize the app | On your device |

We cannot see, access, or recover any of this data — it never reaches us.

## 4. Device permissions we use

HabitHatch requests a small number of Android permissions, used **only** for the features described:

- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) — to show your habit reminders.
- **Exact alarms** (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) — to deliver reminders at the time you set.
- **Run at startup** (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) — to re‑schedule your reminders after your device restarts.
- **Vibration** (`VIBRATE`) — to vibrate for reminders if your device settings allow it.

HabitHatch does **not** access your microphone, camera, contacts, location, photos, calendar, or call/SMS data.

## 5. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and shown **locally by your device**. They are not sent through, or seen by, any server. You can turn reminders off at any time from the app or from your device's notification settings.

## 6. Internet and network

HabitHatch is designed to work fully offline. The app does not send your data over the internet. If a future version adds an online feature (for example, an optional backup or referral system), this policy will be updated **before** that feature ships, and any such feature will be clearly optional.

## 7. Third‑party services

HabitHatch is built with the Expo / React Native framework and uses on‑device libraries for storage, animation, and notifications. These libraries run locally on your device and are **not** used to collect or transmit your personal data. The app contains **no** advertising networks, no analytics providers, and no social‑media tracking.

## 8. Purchases (HabitHatch+)

HabitHatch offers an optional paid subscription, **HabitHatch+**. All purchases are processed by **Google Play Billing**. We never receive or store your payment details — Google handles them under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app only ever learns whether your subscription is currently active (an on/off entitlement) so it can unlock the paid features; it does not receive your name, card, or any personally identifying payment information.

## 9. Children's privacy

HabitHatch does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for general audiences. Because no data is collected or transmitted, the app does not knowingly gather information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

## 10. Data security

Your data is stored on your own device and protected by your device's own security (lock screen, OS sandboxing). Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no server‑side database of your information that could be breached.

## 11. Deleting your data

You are always in control of your data:
- **Reset in‑app:** use the "Reset all data" option in the app to wipe every habit, your companion, and all progress and start over.
- **Uninstall:** removing HabitHatch from your device permanently deletes all of its local data.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice within the app. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
